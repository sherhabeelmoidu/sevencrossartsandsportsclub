OUR HOME

From Kinalur,
with pride.

Nestled in the green foothills of Kozhikode, Kinalur is a place shaped by learning, faith, nature and a close-knit community.

SEVEN CROSS grows from the energy of our hometown—where neighbours meet, young people learn and every shared effort can make a difference.

Our village is home to places of worship, schools and a college, the KSIDC Industrial Growth Centre, and quiet natural spaces that reflect the character of Kinalur. They are the places that connect our people and inspire the club's work.
